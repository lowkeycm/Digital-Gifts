# Meta static concepts — V1 launch

These are concept-ready, not publication-ready. The live destination must match the offer before launch.

## MA-H1-A — Direct

**Primary text**
You shouldn't have to pay and hope the song feels personal.

Tell us the real story, hear a personalized preview, then decide if you want the full song.

**Headline A:** Hear Their Song Before You Pay
**Headline B:** Your Story. Their Preview.
**Description:** Personalized from your details
**CTA:** Create your song

**On-asset:** Hear their name first.

## MA-H2-A — Question

**Primary text**
What do you buy the person who says they don't need anything?

Not another generic gift. Give them something built from the memories, jokes and little details only the two of you know.

**Headline A:** For the Person Who Wants Nothing
**Headline B:** Give Them Something Only You Know
**Description:** One story. One song.
**CTA:** Create your song

**On-asset:** What do you buy them?

## MA-H3-A — Proof / demonstration

**Primary text**
"She steals my fries every time and still says she didn't want any."

That is better song material than "she's amazing."

Give us the details that sound like your relationship. We turn those details into the song.

**Headline A:** The Detail That Makes It Theirs
**Headline B:** Specific In. Personal Out.
**Description:** Built from the real story
**CTA:** Create your preview

**On-asset:** This became the song.

## MA-H4-A — Contrarian

**Primary text**
Don't write lyrics.

Don't make it poetic.

Tell us where you met, the dumb joke you still use, the trip that went sideways, the thing they always steal from your plate.

That's the material.

**Headline A:** Tell Us the Weird Little Stuff
**Headline B:** You Don't Need to Write a Song
**Description:** Just tell the real story
**CTA:** Start your story

**On-asset:** Don't write a song.
