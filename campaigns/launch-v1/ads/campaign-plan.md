# Launch V1 ad campaign plan

Status: concept / pre-production. No ad account actions are authorized or implied.

## Product
$29 personalized-song V1. Guided personal-story intake, personalized preview, private gift page and one revision. Actual music generation and payment are still integration work, so these ads are not publication-ready until the live offer matches them.

## Audience moment
A gift buyer who wants something more personal than another physical gift but does not think of themselves as musical or creative.

## Core creative hypothesis
The category is saturated with "turn your memories into a song." Digital Gifts should make **specificity visible**. The ad should prove that one odd, concrete relationship detail can survive into the song.

## Destination
The create-song funnel. Ad promise and landing page must both preserve:
- preview before final commitment
- $29 offer once payment is actually connected
- raw, specific story details
- one revision

## Proposed measures once live
CTR and landing-page intake start rate are early diagnostics. The meaningful funnel is ad click -> intake complete -> preview heard -> purchase. CPA/ROAS targets remain UNSET until actual margin, music generation cost, refund rate and payment economics are known.
